Existing solutions typically employ a static model partitioning strategy that limits flexibility and optimization in terms of security and performance trade-offs. FlexGE addresses this limitation by allowing users to define customizable partition policies and protection primitives at build time, enabling tailored configurations that adapt to specific use cases. By leveraging a combination of TEE and GPU resources, FlexGE facilitates the secure delegation of computations and enhances the overall robustness of DNN models against potential attacks. The evaluation of FlexGE demonstrates its ability to outperform existing state-of-the-art solutions in both security and performance metrics. The authors showcase the system's flexibility through extensive experiments with various DNN architectures, illustrating how it can navigate the complex configuration space of model partitioning. The incorporation of techniques like Configuration Space Layout Randomization (CSLR) further strengthens the security framework, ensuring that the model remains resilient against adversarial threats. Overall, FlexGE presents a significant advancement in the secure deployment of deep learning models in untrusted environments, highlighting the importance of adaptable security measures in modern AI applications.

This paper represents the first systematic investigation into the security of the AAOS framework, focusing on auto feature checks and access control anomalies.
